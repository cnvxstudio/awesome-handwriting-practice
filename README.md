# Awesome Handwriting Practice

A small, curated collection of resources for preparing a paper handwriting activity: choose a name, a few familiar words, or one short sentence; then pick a worksheet format that leaves room to write.

This list groups resources by the job they help with. It is not a ranking or a claim that one handwriting style suits every learner. Follow a teacher's existing letter models when that matters.

## Worksheet makers and printable collections

- [CreatePrintables: Word Tracing Practice](https://www.createprintables.com/word-tracing-practice-worksheet/) — A word-list format for supplying your own short text.
- [HandwritingWorksheets.com](https://handwritingworksheets.com/) — A worksheet maker with separate print, D'Nealian and cursive routes, plus letter-size and color settings.

## Planning a short activity

- [Handwriting Activity Planner Skill](https://github.com/cnvxstudio/handwriting-activity-planner/blob/main/SKILL.md) — A reusable text-planning checklist for names, word sets and sentences. It provides suggested settings and an example; the text can also be prepared by hand. Maintainer-affiliated.

Three small activities work with the resources above:

1. **Name:** Use one invented first name, such as Mia. Check the spelling and capitalization. Leave a separate line for one independent copy.
2. **Words:** Pick three to five familiar words. For an outdoor theme, try leaf, tree, bird and rain, one word per line. If practising a particular letter or join, check that each word includes it.
3. **Sentence:** Write one short original sentence, such as "The bird is on the tree." Keep the capital letter, punctuation and word spaces.

Choose one focus for the page: letter height, spaces between words, or copying a chosen word. A short page can be more convenient to prepare and check than a large mixed worksheet pack.

## A useful print check

- Check every name, word and line break in the preview.
- Match the selected paper size to the printer.
- Make one test print before printing more copies.
- Check that any light tracing model remains visible on paper.
- Leave enough space for a fresh copy rather than filling every line with models.
- Use a fictional name when sharing examples or requesting help.

For the affiliated PDF maker, initial settings to try are 40 pt with four-line guides for a name, or 32 pt with ruled guides for short words or a sentence. Adjust after previewing. Its repeat control is on/off; it is not a numeric repeat-count setting.

## Selection and contributions

Resources are included for a clear handwriting task and a direct official resource link. This initial list was checked against the publishers' publicly visible resource pages on November 3, 2026; it is not a full functional test of every linked worksheet. Publishers control their own prices, access conditions and permitted uses.

Suggestions should identify the resource, its official URL and the specific activity it supports. Keep descriptions factual, and disclose any ownership or commercial relationship. A concise addition that fills a different need is more useful than a long promotional description.
